Privacy

Short version: your writing never leaves your computer.

What Writeshaper does with your text

It reads the text you select or type, sends it to the writing model running on your own computer (a program called llama.cpp that only listens on your machine), and shows you the result. The text is not saved to disk, not logged and not sent anywhere.

When Writeshaper uses the internet

Once, to download the writing model from Hugging Face when you ask it to. Once a day, to check GitHub for a newer version number, unless you turn this off in Settings. Neither request includes anything you wrote or anything about you.

Your clipboard

To grab your selection and paste the result, Writeshaper uses the clipboard for a moment and then puts back what was on it before.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. The demo on the home page plays answers saved into the page; nothing you do on it is sent anywhere.
