Everything it does

A private writing assistant that lives in your menu bar or tray, works in every app, and never uploads a word.

Works in every app

One shortcut over Mail, Word, Slack, a browser. Select, press, and Replace pastes it back.

Fixes as you type

The Write tab underlines mistakes live and explains each fix. Accept one, or fix them all at once.

Change the tone

Clearer, shorter, friendlier or more professional, in one click. Or tell it what you want, like “make it a bullet list”.

Translate

Turn a message into any of 20 languages, written the way a native speaker would.

Private by design

The model runs on your computer. Nothing you write is uploaded, stored or used to train anything.

Works offline

On a plane, on a train, with Wi-Fi off. After the first download it never needs the internet.

Better writing, everywhere you type.

Free for Mac and Windows. Installer about 111 MB, then a one-time 1.9 GB writing model.
